converted to limud.pb (see LimudSchedules.kt), and the aliyot of hebcal-leyning (https://github.com/hebcal/hebcal-leyning,
same license), in ShnayimMikraWidget.kt; a few typos of the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch schedule were fixed against
the library's simanim.
