The model of the semantic search moved on 2026-11-05 to its own repository, [efrul/pardes-semantic](https://github.com/efrul/pardes-semantic), with this folder's history. Its pipeline, evaluations and the trained models (its releases) are there. What stays here is what Pardes itself reads:
- `NOTICE.md`, the model's credit that Pardes for Android shows in its licences (a copy of pardes-semantic's: change both);
- `release_notes.py`, the pack releases' title and notes.
